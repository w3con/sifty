## Outcome and behavior authority

The owner can prioritize saved vacancies by how they fit the owner's goals, inspect a separate honest assessment of published employer requirements, and see concrete matching, mismatching, missing-profile and unresolved-condition rows. Targeted extraction recovers supported facts from available vacancy text and automatically updates both directions. Demonstrate the complete path on real saved vacancies in staging, including one useful extraction and one completed attempt with no additional facts. The enduring direction is [Quick Match specification section 13](../../../../docs/specs/quick-match.md#13-agreed-next-direction--dual-fit-and-targeted-extraction-2026-11-10-not-implemented); the numerical/runtime policy in this plan is now owner-approved for implementation but remains uncalibrated and not implemented until the relevant stages pass.

## Findings that affect the proposed policy

The existing `quickmatch/professional.go` gives a successfully evidenced skill utility 1 and leaves absent profile evidence, unsupported years and unsupported levels unknown. Simply moving that group to a second score would yield mostly 100 or unscored. The stored profile does not establish a skill's absence from silence. This is why the requirements interval below is a new proposal requiring an owner decision, rather than a mechanical split of the existing five weights.

The existing `server.EnrichJob` and source/fetch adapters already retrieve a single vacancy description; missing text needs that acquisition path, not a model guessing from tags. `matchsvc` currently expects the deterministic extractor's identity and `match_job_facts` holds one current fact set per job. Persisting model output as an alternate extractor would otherwise be overwritten or rejected on the next recalculation. The proposed bounded enrichment layer addresses this product persistence defect; it is not a general plugin or workflow system.

## Product and numerical policy — owner-approved for implementation

1. **Goal fit:** one 0–100 weighted mean over comparable saved goals. Keep existing comparator scales and relative weights for role 25, compensation 20, location preference 15 and conditions 10, normalized over comparable active groups. Skills and legal eligibility are not goal preferences. Keep compensation's declared-ceiling/minimum comparison and existing unknown handling. No new semantic role matcher or inference of desired work from the owner's skills. Unsupported role-content, experience or time-zone comparisons remain explicitly unknown rather than fabricated. Coverage is separate and never a multiplier. This is a proposed starting policy, not measured preference calibration.
2. **Requirement fit:** show a supported interval rather than one falsely precise number. For the deduplicated, explicitly published requirements that have been extracted, retain required/preferred weights 2:1. Confirmed matches contribute utility 1, confirmed incompatibilities 0, and unconfirmed/unsupported comparisons contribute the interval [0,1]. The lower endpoint is the weighted confirmed-match share; the upper endpoint also includes unresolved weight. For example, six confirmed equal-weight requirements plus four unresolved produce 60–100, not a claim of a confirmed 60-point fit. When no comparison is supported, show “Requirements not yet verified” rather than an uninformative 0–100. Include explicit work-authorization eligibility here; do not interpret residence as authorization. Bounds describe extracted requirements only; unresolved extraction flags keep the assessment explicitly provisional, and quotes do not prove complete extraction. Do not rank or penalize a role using the lower endpoint. No third aggregate fit score.
3. **Catalogue order:** sort by current goal-fit raw value descending, then stable job ID. Keep both directions visible in the vacancy panel and a compact pair in the catalogue. Stale/unscored values remain distinguishable and visible, not numeric zero; preserve their explicit state ordering rather than quietly hiding them. Coverage and requirement uncertainty do not break ties as implicit suitability penalties.
4. **Insufficient source:** do not display a numeric fit for a missing/error/pending-detail or metadata-only description, or text consisting only of the stored title/tags. Show “Full job description is unavailable” and reuse the existing single-vacancy refresh action. Do not apply an arbitrary minimum character count or demand a Requirements heading. A genuine short description can still be evaluated. No automatic mass recrawl; normal collection continues to use its existing detail-resolution limits. A successful single-vacancy refresh restarts local extraction and scoring; unavailable sources remain visible and unscored.
5. **Extraction queue:** admit usable text with actionable, deduplicated, unattempted gap kinds; no score threshold. Proposed priority: goal-fit band of 20 points, then distinct gap impact, then raw goal fit, enqueue time and job ID. Unscored goal fit has no fabricated score and joins the waiting queue. Suggested configurable gap weights: requirements and hiring/authorization restrictions 3 each, compensation 2, work conditions 1; one flag per kind, not per warning or bullet. Every fifth dispatch selects the oldest eligible job to avoid permanent starvation. Optional selected/opened-role boosts are excluded from this version. These constants are proposed policy defaults in the existing runtime configuration, not values embedded in worker/view code.
6. **Runtime model use:** reuse the provider selected by existing `providers.stages.extraction`, without choosing or replacing its model, endpoint or credentials. Reuse the existing `assess.InferInto` transport with a vacancy-only typed prompt/schema and extraction thinking disabled where supported. Proposed defaults: one worker, at most 20 physical provider requests per UTC day including failures, and at most three requests per input/producer identity with bounded transient retries. Successful no-new-fact attempts finish permanently for that identity. A changed profile or scoring rule only re-scores; it never earns another extraction call. Changing source or extraction-production identity can make a new attempt eligible, under the same daily cap. Do not silently fall back to another provider. Start the new queue paused; acceptance may enable it on staging with an additional cap of three real provider requests total, and it is paused again afterward. Production queue enablement and production release need the owner's later release decision. Provider price is currently unknown, so request caps are not represented as a monetary cap.

## Data, logic, presentation and architecture

- **Authoritative data:** the existing confirmed typed profile and revision, retained vacancy source and hash, source-policy configuration, versioned vacancy facts and validated extraction evidence. No profile content is sent to vacancy extraction. Treat vacancy text as input data, never instructions for the model to execute tools or score the candidate.
- **Domain:** extend `matchcontract` with the two-direction result, interval/verification states, concrete gap flags, paired conflict evidence and fact-production identity. Pure `jobextract` still does deterministic extraction/gap detection, and pure `quickmatch` performs comparisons only. Keep dependencies on the domain contract, not on each other's implementation, store, provider or view.
- **Persisted enrichment:** add a small source/producer-keyed persisted enrichment/attempt layer through the existing store/migrations. Keep deterministic base facts distinct from validated additional facts; construct an effective fact set with a stable hash for evaluation. Re-scoring must reuse enrichment instead of overwriting it. Changed source invalidates its overlay; stale in-flight output cannot overwrite a newer source or newer production. Validate typed values and exact supporting quotes; unsupported or contradictory facts remain unknown/conflicting, not silent replacements.
- **Effects:** existing `matchsvc`, application composition and background-worker adapters own configuration snapshots, extraction, persistence, atomic claims, retries, cancellation, provider effects, effective-fact assembly, rescore triggers and API projections. Reuse the existing inference lock where necessary to avoid overlapping configured-provider work. The dedicated vacancy-extraction job kind/state must not reuse detailed-assessment queue rows, candidate contexts or enable switches.
- **Presentation:** `frontend/quickmatch.ts`, `QuickMatchPanel.tsx` and the existing catalogue render stored server projections and named callbacks. Presentation may format counts/intervals, but never evaluates fit or schedules a model implicitly on render. Remove the agreed diagnostic clutter; retain provenance internally, pair actual conflicting statements, mark partial matches and separate extraction-pending from genuinely unknown conditions. The existing single-vacancy refresh action and an explicit retry/actionable extraction status provide the adoption path; no new settings framework or administrative dashboard.

The enrichment layer is an explicit architectural change needed to preserve recovered facts across normal profile saves and re-scoring. Allow approximately 10–15 minutes for this separation within Q.3's budget, not as an additional unestimated step. Existing provider transport, path resolution, configuration and application structure remain the reuse points.

Preserve profile revisions/documents/facts, jobs/dates, workflow/Apply history and historical model assessments. Preserve prior Quick Match values until a validated replacement is ready, with the normal migration/recovery snapshot; this does not introduce an unlimited score-history product.
